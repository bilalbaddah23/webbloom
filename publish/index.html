Questions

Good to know.

Do I need to stop using Instagram?

No. Keep Instagram for reaching people, and use the website as your shop. You just add the link to your bio.

What do you need from me?

Only your Instagram page. We take the photos and details from there, and you tell us what to change.

Can I change the design?

Yes. Colours, photos, texts and layout are made the way you want before the site goes live.

How do customers order?

They choose their pieces and send the order to your WhatsApp or Instagram in one tap. You confirm and deliver as you do today.

How long does it take?

The demo is usually ready in a couple of days. After you approve it, the site goes live on your own domain.
